# Frontend Design

Guidance for distinctive, intentional visual design when building new UI or reshaping existing ones.

## Core Philosophy

Approach design work as a creative lead at a boutique studio. The brief emphasizes avoiding templated defaults through deliberate, opinionated choices in palette, typography, and layout, while justifying "one real aesthetic risk."

## Key Sections

**Ground it in the subject:** Identify the concrete subject, its audience, and the page's purpose before designing. Draw inspiration from the subject's own materials, instruments, and language rather than generic patterns.

**Design principles:** Focus on five areas—a memorable hero thesis, typography as personality, structure that encodes information, deliberate motion use, and complexity matching the vision. The guidance warns against three common AI-generated defaults: cream backgrounds with serif/terracotta, dark backgrounds with neon accents, and broadsheet-style layouts.

**Process approach:** Work in two passes—first create a compact token system (color, type, layout, signature), then review against the brief to ensure uniqueness before building code.

**Writing guidance:** Copy should clarify understanding and ease navigation. Use active voice, plain language, conversational register, and consistency in vocabulary. Treat errors and empty states as navigation opportunities.

**Implementation notes:** Exercise restraint by concentrating boldness in one signature element. Maintain responsive design, keyboard accessibility, and respect for reduced-motion preferences.
